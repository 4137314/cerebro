#+TITLE: Inner product space
#+DATE: [2026-07-29 Wed 13:55]
#+FILETAGS: :evergreen:medium:linear_algebra:
#+MSC: 15A63

An /inner product/ equips a real [[id:7c54b427-4d7a-4511-b374-58c7bb44fb84][vector space]] with lengths and angles,
turning linear algebra into geometry.

#+DEPENDS: def-vector-space
#+NAME: def-inner-product-space
#+begin_definition
A *(real) inner product space* is a [[id:7c54b427-4d7a-4511-b374-58c7bb44fb84][vector space]] \(V\) over \(\R\)
together with a map \(\inner{\cdot}{\cdot} \colon V \times V \to \R\) that
is /symmetric/ (\(\inner{u}{v} = \inner{v}{u}\)), /bilinear/ (linear in
each argument), and /positive definite/ (\(\inner{v}{v} \ge 0\), with
equality iff \(v = 0\)).
#+end_definition

The associated *norm* is \(\norm{v} = \sqrt{\inner{v}{v}}\), and \(u, v\)
are /orthogonal/ when \(\inner{u}{v} = 0\).
