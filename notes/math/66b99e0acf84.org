#+TITLE: Vector space

A /vector space/ endows an [[id:c982302e-b3f8-40d7-b38d-73177ae644d4][abelian group]] with a compatible action of a
field of scalars -- the arena of linear algebra and the local model of
every geometry considered here.

#+DEPENDS: def-abelian-group
#+NAME: def-vector-space
#+begin_definition
Let \(K\) be a field. A *vector space* over \(K\) is an [[id:c982302e-b3f8-40d7-b38d-73177ae644d4][abelian group]]
\((V, +)\) together with a *scalar multiplication* \(K \times V \to V\),
\((\lambda, v) \mapsto \lambda v\), such that for all \(\lambda, \mu \in K\)
and \(u, v \in V\):
1. \(\lambda (u + v) = \lambda u + \lambda v\);
2. \((\lambda + \mu) v = \lambda v + \mu v\);
3. \((\lambda \mu) v = \lambda (\mu v)\);
4. \(1\, v = v\), where \(1\) is the multiplicative identity of \(K\).
#+end_definition

Elements of \(V\) are /vectors/, elements of \(K\) /scalars/. The running
example is \(K = \R\) and \(V = \R^{n}\).
