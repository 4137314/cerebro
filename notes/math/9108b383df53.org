* Continuity at a point

#+NAME: def-continuity-at-point
#+begin_definition
Let \(f\colon D \to \R\) with \(D \subseteq \R\) and let \(x_0 \in D\). Then
\(f\) is /continuous/ at \(x_0\) if for every \(\eps > 0\) there is a
\(\delta > 0\) such that \(\abs{f(x) - f(x_0)} < \eps\) whenever \(x \in D\)
and \(\abs{x - x_0} < \delta\).
#+end_definition

#+begin_comment
The theorem below rests on the definition above: =#+DEPENDS:= records that
as data (see .emacs.d/lisp/cerebro-results.el). It is invisible to every
export profile -- Org drops unknown keywords, like #+MSC: -- and only

#+end_comment
#+DEPENDS: def-continuity-at-point
#+NAME: thm-sum-of-continuous
#+begin_theorem
If \(f, g \colon D \to \R\) are continuous at \(x_0\), then so is \(f + g\).
#+end_theorem

#+begin_proof
Fix \(\eps > 0\). By continuity of \(f\) and \(g\) at \(x_0\) there are
\(\delta_1, \delta_2 > 0\) with \(\abs{f(x) - f(x_0)} < \eps/2\) for
\(\abs{x - x_0} < \delta_1\) and \(\abs{g(x) - g(x_0)} < \eps/2\) for
\(\abs{x - x_0} < \delta_2\). Put \(\delta = \min(\delta_1, \delta_2)\).
Then for \(\abs{x - x_0} < \delta\),
\[\abs{(f+g)(x) - (f+g)(x_0)} \leq \abs{f(x) - f(x_0)} + \abs{g(x) - g(x_0)} < \eps,\]
so \(f + g\) is continuous at \(x_0\).
#+end_proof

#+DEPENDS: def-continuity-at-point, cor-reverse-triangle-inequality
#+NAME: prop-absolute-value-continuous
#+begin_proposition
The function \(x \mapsto \abs{x}\) is continuous at every point of
\(\R\), and \(\delta = \eps\) suffices at every point.
#+end_proposition

#+begin_proof
Let \(x_{0} \in \R\) and \(\eps > 0\), and take \(\delta = \eps\). For
\(\abs{x - x_{0}} < \delta\) the reverse triangle inequality gives
\(\bigl| \abs{x} - \abs{x_{0}} \bigr| \le \abs{x - x_{0}} < \eps\).
#+end_proof

#+begin_remark
Continuity is a local property: it is defined point by point, and a
function can be continuous at some points of its domain and not others.
The proposition above is the case where the local statement happens to
hold uniformly -- \(\delta\) does not depend on \(x_{0}\) -- which is
the distinction uniform continuity is about.
#+end_remark
