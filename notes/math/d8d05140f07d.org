#+TITLE: Algebraic topology
#+DATE: [2026-08-10 Mon]
#+FILETAGS: :growing:topology:algebraic_topology:
#+MSC: 55-XX
#+HASH: d8d05140f07d

Connectedness and compactness distinguish spaces by a yes or a no, and
they cannot tell a disc from an annulus. Algebraic topology assigns to a
space an algebraic object instead, functorially, so that a continuous
map becomes a homomorphism -- and a geometric impossibility becomes an
algebraic one, which is a kind of statement one can actually prove.

Part of [[id:830a0aed-a379-4350-bd58-f776af9b87d7][Topology]].

* The construction

[[id:e1b6a239-2261-4132-9261-3152bd0c001f][Path homotopy and the fundamental group]]: loops at a point, up to
deformation, under concatenation. The definition is forced -- with no
quotient by homotopy the operation is not even associative -- and
functoriality is what makes it useful.

[[id:a5a339a4-6ca9-4440-9072-8751f2f69c66][Homotopy equivalence, retracts and contractible spaces]] identifies the
equivalence the invariant actually sees, which is coarser than
homeomorphism: a disc and a point are the same to it. That is a loss and
a gain at once, and it is what makes computation possible, since a space
may be replaced by any homotopy equivalent one.

* The computation

[[id:6264edf2-9cd7-4236-9d29-31f35613f8ec][Covering spaces and the fundamental group of the circle]] is the first
group actually computed, and it needs an idea the general theory does
not supply: lift a loop to the line, where the only invariant left is
where the lifted path ends. The winding number is that endpoint, and
\(\pi_{1}(S^{1}) \iso \Z\) is the whole subject in miniature -- from
which the non-existence of a retraction of the disc onto its boundary,
and Brouwer's fixed point theorem in dimension two, follow in a
paragraph.

* What is missing

Seifert-van Kampen, which is how any other fundamental group gets
computed; the classification of covering spaces and its correspondence
with subgroups of \(\pi_{1}\); higher homotopy groups; and homology,
which is what replaces \(\pi_{1}\) above dimension one and which the
whole area is waiting for -- spheres of dimension at least two are
simply connected, so nothing here distinguishes them.
