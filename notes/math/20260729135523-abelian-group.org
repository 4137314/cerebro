#+TITLE: Abelian group
#+DATE: [2026-07-29 Wed 13:55]

An /abelian group/ is a [[id:c74536f9-f1a8-4ce8-a26f-8ae6300aa2eb][group]] whose operation ignores the order of its
arguments -- the additive skeleton of rings, fields, and vector spaces.

#+DEPENDS: def-group
#+NAME: def-abelian-group
#+begin_definition
A [[id:c74536f9-f1a8-4ce8-a26f-8ae6300aa2eb][group]] \((G, \ast)\) is *abelian* (or *commutative*) if
\[
  a \ast b = b \ast a \qquad \text{for all } a, b \in G .
\]
The operation of an abelian group is usually written additively, \(a + b\),
with identity \(0\) and inverse \(-a\).
#+end_definition
