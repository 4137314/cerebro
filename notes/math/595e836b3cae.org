#+TITLE: Limit of a sequence
#+DATE: [2026-08-06 Thu]

#+NAME: def-sequence-limit
#+begin_definition
A sequence \((a_{n})_{n \in \N}\) of real numbers /converges/ to
\(L \in \R\) if for every \(\eps > 0\) there is \(N \in \N\) such that
\(\abs{a_{n} - L} < \eps\) for all \(n \ge N\). One writes
\(\lim_{n \to \infty} a_{n} = L\), and calls \(L\) the /limit/ of the
sequence.
#+end_definition

#+DEPENDS: def-sequence-limit
#+NAME: thm-limit-unique
#+begin_theorem
A convergent sequence has exactly one limit.
#+end_theorem

#+begin_proof
Suppose \(a_{n} \to L\) and \(a_{n} \to M\) with \(L \ne M\), and put
\(\eps = \abs{L - M}/2 > 0\). Choose \(N_{1}\) with
\(\abs{a_{n} - L} < \eps\) for \(n \ge N_{1}\) and \(N_{2}\) with
\(\abs{a_{n} - M} < \eps\) for \(n \ge N_{2}\). For
\(n \ge \max(N_{1}, N_{2})\) the triangle inequality gives
\[ \abs{L - M} \le \abs{L - a_{n}} + \abs{a_{n} - M} < 2\eps = \abs{L - M}, \]
which is absurd. Hence \(L = M\).
#+end_proof

#+NAME: def-bounded-sequence
#+begin_definition
A sequence \((a_{n})\) is /bounded/ if there is \(C > 0\) with
\(\abs{a_{n}} \le C\) for every \(n\), and /monotone increasing/ if
\(a_{n} \le a_{n+1}\) for every \(n\).
#+end_definition
