#+TITLE: Set theory

Two thirds of this subject is not foundational at all but a toolbox: the
quotient construction, the maximality principle and the induction that
runs past \(\N\) are used constantly elsewhere, and are collected here
because they are about sets and nothing else. The remaining third --
cardinality -- is where sets become objects of study rather than
instruments.

Part of [[id:1ba699a0-80ee-42e3-ae2b-bfbbc0a2c6a8][Foundations]].

* The constructions everything else uses

[[id:87a33b59-f21b-476c-9716-29b02b4981d3][Relations, equivalence and the quotient of a set]] is the single most
reused note in the archive: every later quotient -- of a group, a ring, a
vector space, a topological space, a metric space -- is this
construction plus a check that the structure is constant on classes.
[[id:da2475a4-40d1-48a3-8f06-cef2ef4ad52a][Maps, invertibility and the canonical factorisation]] is the same
statement for functions, and the isomorphism theorems of [[id:504cda55-cfbf-42c2-b481-8d90660ec02a][algebra]] are it
with structure added.

* Order

[[id:e806603a-321a-499a-96cb-860e41617a87][Partial orders, lattices and the Knaster-Tarski theorem]] shows how much
of the theory of the supremum survives without an order that is total or
complete -- enough to produce fixed points, with no analysis anywhere.
[[id:7560e120-7246-45b6-ac7a-998bc9f39136][Well-ordered sets and transfinite induction]] and [[id:b79e44e5-70dd-48a2-a1b2-9f635e55ba6b][ordinals and definition
by transfinite recursion]] separate two things usually run together:
proving along a well order, and building along one.

* Choice

[[id:3cb0cea8-d46a-44cf-ad4f-39b88f22d50e][The axiom of choice and Zorn's lemma]] is the export product of the
subject. It is used in this archive to produce a basis of an arbitrary
vector space, a maximal ideal, a Hahn-Banach extension, a maximal
consistent set of formulas and an ultrafilter; every one of those
objects exists and none can be exhibited.
[[id:e3c053e0-2462-4692-8b0d-ee81002ec63c][Filters, ultrafilters and the ultrafilter lemma]] package the last of
these, and [[id:22a8386a-16ba-435b-a71d-26d9886886d0][Boolean algebras and Stone's representation theorem]] show
what they are: the points of a space built out of an algebra of
propositions.

* Size

[[id:1ef16512-bb90-4204-bd8e-9f75293df0cc][Cardinality: Cantor's theorem and Cantor-Schroder-Bernstein]] makes
comparison by injections behave like a notion of size -- antisymmetric,
and unbounded above. The two proofs are worth contrasting: the
diagonal argument is three lines and completely explicit, the
Schroder-Bernstein argument is a fixed point of a monotone operator on a
power set.

* What is missing

The axioms themselves -- there is no note stating ZF, so "set" is used
here as an undefined term with properties assumed as needed. Also
absent: cardinal arithmetic, Hartogs' theorem, the continuum hypothesis
and independence, and the cumulative hierarchy.
