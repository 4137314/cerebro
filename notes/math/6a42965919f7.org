#+TITLE: Field theory
#+DATE: [2026-08-10 Mon]

A field extension is a vector space, and that single observation makes
the subject finite: questions about which numbers can be adjoined become
questions about dimensions, and dimensions multiply along towers. The
classical impossibility results are that multiplication and nothing
else.

Part of [[id:504cda55-cfbf-42c2-b481-8d90660ec02a][Algebra]].

* The three notes

[[id:06b49b70-2706-41e5-b4e0-612723e3adcf][Field extensions, the tower law and minimal polynomials]] is the
foundation: the degree is multiplicative, an algebraic element generates
an extension isomorphic to a quotient of a polynomial ring, and its
degree is the degree of one irreducible polynomial.

[[id:d61e9b83-1185-46ed-9977-48c786495d24][Splitting fields and their uniqueness]] constructs, for any polynomial,
a smallest field in which it factors completely, and -- the substantial
half -- shows that field does not depend on the choices made. That
uniqueness is what makes "the" splitting field, and hence the Galois
group, a well-defined object.

[[id:b9ac706e-3183-46fd-84d2-6982c113d46f][Finite fields]] is the complete classification the two previous notes
make possible: one field of each prime-power order, none of any other
size, and a cyclic multiplicative group.

* What this subject is waiting for

Every note here is a prerequisite of Galois theory and none of them is
Galois theory. What is missing is the correspondence itself: separable
and normal extensions, the automorphism group of a splitting field, and
the theorem that its subgroups match the intermediate fields. With
[[id:68aecb66-5e63-4fa6-bfad-5629a7a343d2][solvable groups]] already in place, the insolubility of the general
quintic is two notes away -- and the counting argument that begins it is
sketched in the remark at the end of the splitting-field note.

Also absent: algebraic closures, whose existence needs a transfinite
iteration of [[id:d61e9b83-1185-46ed-9977-48c786495d24][the splitting-field construction]]; transcendence degree; and
the theory of separability in positive characteristic, of which only
[[id:b9ac706e-3183-46fd-84d2-6982c113d46f][the Frobenius map]] appears.
