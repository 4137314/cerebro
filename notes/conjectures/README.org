#+TITLE: Conjectures

Mine. Statements I believe and cannot prove, written down so that
"things I am actually stuck on" is a question with an answer instead of
a feeling.

A conjecture is a different /kind/ of writing from a result, which is
why it has a directory rather than a tag: a theorem is finished and a
conjecture is an argument you keep having with yourself for years. Apart
from that they are ordinary notes -- hash-named (=cerebro-hash.el= owns
this directory exactly like =notes/math/=), carrying an =:ID:=, read by
the results graph, so a note in =math/= may cite one and
=cerebro results open= counts it among what the archive owes.

* Making one

: C-c m j          a conjecture note, here, born tagged :conjecture:

The tag is not decoration: =bin/notes-lint.el= exempts a statement
tagged =conjecture= from the "every theorem needs an adjacent proof"
rule, which is precisely what an unproved claim of your own needs.

Write the statement in a =#+begin_conjecture= block with a =#+NAME:=
(=conj-...=), and use =#+DEPENDS:= for the results it would rest on if
it were true. That is what makes a conjecture a node: =cerebro results
dependents conj-something= then answers "what would follow".

* Printing them

: make export FILE=notes/conjectures/<hash>.org PROFILE=conjecture

The =conjecture= profile is a *two-column article* -- cerebro.cls with
KOMA's =twocolumn=, the same class, macros and theorem suite as every
other output here. A page of open questions is a page of short,
self-contained statements, and the narrow measure of two columns is what
that reads best in. Everything else about it is the archive's standard
look: the same linear title block, the same numbering, the same
bibliography.

* Conventions

The ones from [[file:../README.org][notes/README.org]] apply unchanged. Two are worth repeating
here because a conjecture is where they are most tempting to skip:

- *State it precisely enough to be wrong.* A conjecture you cannot
  refute is not a conjecture, it is a mood. Quantifiers explicit,
  hypotheses named.
- *Record what made you believe it* -- the special cases you checked,
  the analogy it came from -- in a =#+begin_remark=. In two years that
  paragraph is the whole value of the file.
