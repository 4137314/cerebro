* Description

A first rigorous course in real analysis: the real numbers, sequences
and series, limits and continuity, differentiation, and the Riemann
integral -- with an emphasis on proof from the \(\eps\)--\(\delta\)
definitions up.

* Objectives

By the end of the course, a student can:
- state and use the completeness of \(\R\);
- prove convergence of sequences and series directly from the
  definitions;
- work with continuity and differentiability rigorously;
- construct and read \(\eps\)--\(\delta\) arguments unaided.

* Schedule

1. The real numbers and completeness.
2. Sequences: convergence, monotone and Cauchy sequences.
3. Series: convergence tests, absolute convergence.
4. Limits and continuity ([[file:01-limits-and-continuity.org][notes]]).
5. Differentiation: the mean value theorem and its consequences.
6. The Riemann integral and the fundamental theorem of calculus.
