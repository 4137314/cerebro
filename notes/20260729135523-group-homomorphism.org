#+TITLE: Group homomorphism
#+DATE: [2026-07-29 Wed 13:55]
#+FILETAGS: :evergreen:easy:group_theory:
#+MSC: 20A05

A /homomorphism/ is a map between [[id:c74536f9-f1a8-4ce8-a26f-8ae6300aa2eb][group]]s that respects their
operations -- the notion of structure-preserving map for groups.

#+DEPENDS: def-group
#+NAME: def-group-homomorphism
#+begin_definition
Let \((G, \ast)\) and \((H, \cdot)\) be [[id:c74536f9-f1a8-4ce8-a26f-8ae6300aa2eb][group]]s. A *homomorphism* from
\(G\) to \(H\) is a function \(f \colon G \to H\) with
\[
  f(a \ast b) = f(a) \cdot f(b) \qquad \text{for all } a, b \in G .
\]
A bijective homomorphism is an *isomorphism*; two groups joined by one are
indistinguishable as groups.
#+end_definition
