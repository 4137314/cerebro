#+title: Convex set
#+summary: Convexity is the most basic shape condition on a subset of a real [[id:vector-space][vector space]]: it must contain the whole segment between any two of its points.
#+date: [2026-07-29 Wed 13:55]

#+DEPENDS: def-vector-space
#+NAME: def-convex-set
#+begin_definition
Let \(V\) be a real [[id:vector-space][vector space]]. A subset \(C \subseteq V\) is
*convex* if for all \(x, y \in C\) and all \(t \in [0, 1]\),
\[
  t x + (1 - t) y \in C .
\]
As \(t\) runs over \([0, 1]\) the point \(t x + (1 - t) y\) traces the
segment from \(x\) to \(y\); convexity says that segment stays inside \(C\).
#+end_definition
