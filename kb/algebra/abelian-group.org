#+title: Abelian group
#+summary: An /abelian group/ is a [[id:group][group]] whose operation ignores the order of its arguments -- the additive skeleton of rings, fields, and vector spaces.
#+date: [2026-07-29 Wed 13:55]

#+DEPENDS: def-group
#+NAME: def-abelian-group
#+begin_definition
A [[id:group][group]] \((G, \ast)\) is *abelian* (or *commutative*) if
\[
  a \ast b = b \ast a \qquad \text{for all } a, b \in G .
\]
The operation of an abelian group is usually written additively, \(a + b\),
with identity \(0\) and inverse \(-a\).
#+end_definition
