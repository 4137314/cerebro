#+title: Mathematics
#+summary: This is the root of the archive: one note per subject, each linking to the notes below it, down to the results themselves. It exists because the two navigations this repository already has answer different questions. =cerebro note list= and =cerebro query by-tag= say what is /there/; the results graph says what /rests on what/. Neither says what the subject is about, which is the only question a person actually asks before opening anything.
#+date: [2026-08-10 Mon]

So a hub note carries no mathematics. It states what its area is, which
threads run through it, which results are load-bearing, and -- as
honestly as possible -- what is missing. Nothing here may be depended on
by a =#+DEPENDS:=, and nothing here is generated: a list that a script
could produce belongs to the script.

* The areas

- [[id:analysis][Analysis]] -- limits, and everything that is a limit in disguise:
  the real line, sequences and series, differentiation and integration,
  measure, and the geometry of infinite-dimensional spaces.
- [[id:algebra][Algebra]] -- sets with operations, studied through the maps that
  respect them: groups, rings, fields, vector spaces and modules.
- [[id:geometry][Geometry]] -- what survives a change of coordinates, once one has
  chosen which changes are allowed: affine, Euclidean, projective,
  convex and differential.
- [[id:topology][Topology]] -- nearness with no distance: what continuity,
  connectedness and compactness mean when only the open sets remain.
- [[id:foundations][Foundations]] -- what the rest is written in: sets, order, choice,
  and the logic that makes "provable" a mathematical notion.
- [[id:probability][Probability]] -- measure theory with total mass one, plus the two
  ideas that are not measure-theoretic at all: independence and
  conditioning. Its applied half is [[id:statistics][Statistics]].
- [[id:mathematical-physics][Mathematical physics]] -- classical mechanics as geometry, from the
  variational principle to the symplectic structure and the rigid body,
  and the Hilbert space formalism of quantum mechanics.
- [[id:dynamical-systems][Dynamical systems]] -- a differential equation read as a flow, and the
  two ways of deciding where it goes without solving it.

* How the areas are wired to each other

The division above is a shelving convention, not a claim about the
mathematics, and the interesting notes are the ones that cross it.
Three crossings carry most of the traffic here:

- *Order and choice into everything.* [[id:the-axiom-of-choice-and-zorns-lemma][Zorn's lemma]] is used to produce a
  [[id:span-linear-independence-bases-and-dimension][basis of an arbitrary vector space]], a [[id:integral-domains-fields-prime-and-maximal-ideals][maximal ideal of any ring]], a
  [[id:the-hahn-banach-theorem][functional extending one on a subspace]] and a [[id:filters-ultrafilters-and-the-ultrafilter-lemma][maximal
  finitely satisfiable set of formulas]] -- four theorems, one argument.
- *Completeness into fixed points.* Completeness of a metric space gives
  the [[id:the-contraction-mapping-principle][contraction principle]], hence [[id:existence-and-uniqueness-for-ordinary-differential-equations][existence for differential
  equations]]; and it gives the [[id:the-baire-category-theorem][Baire category theorem]], hence the
  [[id:uniform-boundedness-open-mapping-and-closed-graph][three theorems of Banach space theory]].
- *Linear algebra into geometry and analysis.* The [[id:the-spectral-theorem-for-symmetric-operators][spectral theorem]] is
  what diagonalises the [[id:surfaces-the-two-fundamental-forms-and-gauss-curvature][shape operator of a surface]], and
  [[id:bilinear-forms-quadratic-forms-and-sylvesters-law-of-inertia][Sylvester's law]] is what classifies the [[id:conics-and-quadrics-classification-by-signature][quadrics]].

* What this archive is not, yet

Named so that the gaps are visible rather than implied: there is no
number theory, no combinatorics, no representation theory, no
homological algebra, and no Galois theory -- though
[[id:splitting-fields-and-their-uniqueness][splitting fields]] and [[id:composition-series-jordan-holder-and-solvable-groups][solvable groups]] are both in place, so the two
halves of it exist without the correspondence between them. Measure
theory stops at the [[id:the-lebesgue-integral-and-the-convergence-theorems][convergence theorems]]: Lebesgue measure itself is
used and never constructed, and the absence of Fubini's theorem is what
leaves two results in [[id:probability][probability]] assumed rather than proved.

#+begin_remark
A hub is maintained by hand and will therefore go stale, which is the
usual argument against writing one. It earns its place only by saying
what cannot be derived -- why a subject is one subject, and which of its
results the others lean on. The moment a section here becomes a list of
titles, the list belongs to =cerebro note list= and the section should
be deleted.
#+end_remark
