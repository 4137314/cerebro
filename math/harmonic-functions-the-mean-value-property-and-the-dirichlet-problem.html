Harmonic functions, the mean value property and the Dirichlet problem

Laplace's equation is the model elliptic equation and the stationary case of both the heat and the wave equations. Its solutions are as rigid as holomorphic functions, for the same reason – in the plane they are exactly the real parts of holomorphic functions – and the mean value property is the statement from which all the rigidity follows.

Existence for the Dirichlet problem is the half not proved here, and it is genuinely harder than uniqueness: it needs either an explicit kernel on a special domain – the Poisson kernel on a disc, which is the harmonic analogue of the Cauchy formula – or Perron's method of subharmonic functions on a general one.
