Ownership, liability and governance

Partnerships give partners unlimited liability, corporations limit it to the capital invested; governance assigns roles to the shareholders' meeting, board, statutory auditors, CEO and general manager.

Definition

Three faces of the same coin: azienda (resources), impresa (entrepreneurial activity), società (legal form of the two). Governance is the set of rules of the firm as an institution (legal subject) by which it is managed and controlled, completing the obligations of laws and binding norms.

Comparison

Form Examples Liability for company debts
Partnership (di persone) S.n.c., S.a.s. unlimited: reaches personal property
Corporation (di capitali) S.p.A., S.r.l. limited to capital invested

Corporations pay for limited liability with greater duties (formalities, publication of accounts, control bodies). In an S.a.s. general partners (accomandatari) have unlimited liability and manage; limited partners (accomandanti) risk only their contribution.

Model

Body Role
Shareholders' meeting ordinary and extraordinary; appoints board
Board of directors (CdA) strategic direction, oversight of management
Board of statutory auditors control and audit, protecting shareholders
CEO (amministratore delegato) executes the investment of shareholders
General manager coordinates the functional areas

Example

Shareholders of an S.p.A. meet and appoint the board; the board appoints the CEO; the CEO with the general manager runs production, sales and finance; the statutory auditors check the accounts. If the firm fails, shareholders lose their capital but not their houses — unlike S.n.c. partners.

Pitfall

Unlimited liability means exposure of personal assets, not unlimited power. Ownership, governance and day-to-day management may belong to different people.

Recall

Classify S.n.c., S.a.s., S.r.l., S.p.A. by liability; list the five governance bodies with one role each.
