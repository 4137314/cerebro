Method (volt-second balance)

Engineering applications

CPU/GPU voltage regulators (multiphase bucks), PFC boost stages, battery chargers, LED drivers, isolated converters (flyback/forward with a transformer), motor inverters (three half-bridges with PWM).

Pitfall

Ideal formulas ignore the diode drop and resistances: at low a diode loses (≈ 0.4·0.725·10 ≈ 3 W in the example) — synchronous rectification with a MOSFET is essential.
