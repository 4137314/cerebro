Worked example

Engineering applications

  • PET: back-to-back 511 keV photons localise the tracer.
  • Radiation shielding and dosimetry: Compton dominates photon attenuation in tissue from ~30 keV to ~25 MeV; above, pair production ( near nuclei) and bremsstrahlung dominate.
  • Inverse Compton sources (tunable X-rays from laser–electron collisions), Compton cameras.

Pitfall

Thomson scattering is energy-independent; Klein–Nishina falls with energy because of recoil and spin: do not use for MeV photons.
