Operation

  • Write: WL high, BL at or 0. An NMOS passes a "1" only up to (threshold drop; body effect makes it worse) unless the word line is boosted above .
  • Read: BL precharged to (often ); WL high → charge sharing; the sense amplifier compares with a reference and restores (writes back) the full level.
  • Refresh: every row read and rewritten every ~64 ms.

Engineering applications

Main memory (DDR, LPDDR, HBM), embedded DRAM caches; retention is set by junction and subthreshold leakage (generation current, SRH), which doubles every ~10 K — refresh rate doubles above 85 °C.

Pitfall

The read signal depends on , not on alone: longer bit lines (more cells) reduce the signal; that is why arrays are segmented.
