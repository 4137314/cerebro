Stability

Poles in the left half-plane → decaying modes; on the axis → oscillation; right half-plane → instability. Feedback amplifiers and oscillators are designed by placing poles (root locus, Nyquist).

Engineering applications

Switching transients (inrush, ringing), control-loop design of power converters and PLLs, step response specs (rise time, overshoot) of amplifiers, transient analysis in SPICE, the Green's-function view of propagators.
