Problem 8 — CMOS NAND3 vs dynamic NAND3

All transistors equal, 20 fF drain capacitance each. Which is slower?

Solution

Static NAND3: output node sees 3 PMOS drains + 1 NMOS drain = 80 fF; pull-down 3 in series (): fF plus internal nodes. Dynamic NAND3: output sees 1 PMOS + 1 NMOS drain = 40 fF, but pull-down has 4 in series (3 + foot): fF = 160 vs 240 (× fF): the dynamic gate is faster and smaller (5 vs 6 transistors), at the price of clocking, charge sharing and precharge power.
