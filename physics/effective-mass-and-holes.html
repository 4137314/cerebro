Holes

In an almost full band, the current of all electrons equals that of a single positive charge moving with the velocity of the missing electron. At the band top : the missing electron has negative mass and charge , the hole has , charge , energy increasing downwards (Heisenberg 1931). Valence bands of Si/GaAs: heavy holes, light holes, split-off band.

Direct and indirect gaps

Direct (GaAs, InP): conduction minimum and valence maximum at the same → efficient light emission. Indirect (Si, Ge): minimum at a different , so a phonon must supply momentum → weak emission, slow absorption edge (optical properties).

Worked example

GaAs : in a field of 1 kV/cm an electron starting at rest gains m/s in 0.1 ps (a typical scattering time). Mobility m²/Vs = 2600 cm²/Vs, the right order of real GaAs (drift).

Engineering applications

Low effective mass → high mobility → fast transistors (InGaAs HEMTs); strain engineering of Si channels reduces and boosts mobility in modern CMOS; hole effective mass sets PMOS sizing (CMOS sizing ratio).
