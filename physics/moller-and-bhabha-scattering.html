Møller scattering

: exchanging the two outgoing electrons swaps (Møller 1932). In the ultrarelativistic limit

Symmetric under (identical particles); the interference term is the quantum signature of indistinguishability.

Bhabha scattering

Diagrams: t-channel (as Møller with one line reversed) and s-channel annihilation , with a relative minus sign (Bhabha 1936). Crossing the Møller result () gives the formula in the summary; in the CM frame

Muon pairs

Worked example

Bhabha at small angle is dominated by the t-channel, : huge and precisely calculable, so colliders use it as the luminosity monitor. At GeV, between 30 and 50 mrad: nb, comparable to the hadronic Z peak (≈ 41 nb): a high, precisely known rate.

Engineering applications

Luminosity calibration, polarimetry of electron beams (Møller polarimeters use the spin dependence), positron-annihilation-based imaging and material analysis.

Pitfall

Forgetting the relative minus sign between direct and exchange diagrams changes the interference term and violates the Pauli principle.
