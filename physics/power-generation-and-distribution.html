Engineering applications

Grid design (generation 10–25 kV, transmission 220–400 kV, distribution 15–20 kV, users 400/230 V), motors (rotating field from three phases), HVDC for very long links and cables, renewable inverters synchronising to the grid.

Pitfall

Line voltage vs phase voltage: 400 V is between two phases, 230 V phase-to-neutral; power formulas with use line quantities.
