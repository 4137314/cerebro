Quantum mechanics: the map of the subject

States are unit vectors (rays) of a complex Hilbert space, observables are self-adjoint operators, probabilities are , and time evolution is the unitary group generated by the Hamiltonian; everything else in the course is a consequence or an application of these four rules.

How to study it

Follow the prerequisite order (`]`). For every note: state the result from memory, redo the proof on paper, then solve the worked example without looking. The exam practice collects full problems; the formula sheet is for the last day.

Engineering applications

Transistors and lasers (solid state, optoelectronics), MRI (spin precession), scanning tunnelling microscopes and flash memory (tunnelling), atomic clocks (hyperfine and Zeeman shifts), superconducting logic (SFQ) and quantum computers (qubits).
