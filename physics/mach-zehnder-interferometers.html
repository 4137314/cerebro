Engineering applications

Modulators (Si, LiNbO), optical switches and routers, wavelength (de)multiplexers and interleavers, sensors (one arm exposed), programmable photonics and optical neural networks (Reck/Clements MZI meshes), quantum photonic circuits.

Pitfall

Path imbalance converts laser phase noise into intensity noise when is comparable to the coherence length (coherence); use balanced MZIs for modulators.
