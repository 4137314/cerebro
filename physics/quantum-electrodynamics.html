Recipe for an exam problem

  1. Draw all diagrams of the lowest order (relative signs for exchanged fermions).
  2. Write with the Feynman rules.
  3. Average over initial spins (), sum over final ones: turn into traces.
  4. Evaluate in the centre-of-mass or lab frame with Mandelstam variables.
  5. Insert in the cross-section formula and check limits (non-relativistic → Rutherford, ).

Engineering applications

QED predicts the electron g-factor (metrology of ), atomic level shifts used by optical clocks, X-ray and gamma interactions with matter (Compton scattering in medical imaging and radiotherapy dosimetry, pair production in PET detectors and shielding), synchrotron light sources.
