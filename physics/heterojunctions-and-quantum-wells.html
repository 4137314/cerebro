Heterojunctions and quantum wells

Joining semiconductors of different gaps creates band offsets (); a narrow-gap layer between wide-gap ones confines carriers (double heterostructure), and if thinner than ~20 nm quantises their motion into subbands with a step-like 2D density of states — the basis of efficient lasers, LEDs and HEMTs.

Engineering applications

Double-heterostructure and QW laser diodes, high-brightness LEDs (InGaN/GaN QWs), HEMTs for 5G and radar, HBTs (wide-gap emitter blocks hole injection), quantum-cascade lasers, QW electro-absorption modulators.

Pitfall

Anderson's rule ignores interface dipoles and is often off by 0.1–0.2 eV; measured offsets should be used for design.
