NMOS inverters: resistive, saturated and depletion loads

An NMOS switch with a pull-up load is a ratioed gate: follows from equating the triode switch current with the load current, and static power is drawn whenever the output is low; resistive loads need large R, saturated (diode-connected) loads lose (with body effect), depletion loads restore and give sharper VTCs.

Engineering applications

Historical NMOS microprocessors (6800, Z80), today's pseudo-NMOS and current-mode logic, analog current mirrors and active loads (same diode-connected devices), sense amplifiers.

Pitfall

In ratioed logic depends on the ratio of switch to load strength; making both transistors bigger by the same factor does not lower , it only increases power.
