Temperature regimes

regime T range (Si, N_D = 10¹⁵) n
freeze-out below ≈ 100 K donors not ionised
extrinsic ≈ 100–500 K n ≈ N_D (flat)
intrinsic above ≈ 550 K n ≈ n_i, rising fast

The extrinsic plateau is the useful operating range; its upper end limits Si devices to ~150–200 °C and motivates wide-gap SiC/GaN (wide-bandgap devices) (Wilson 1931).

Engineering applications

Doping profiles define wells, sources/drains and channels in CMOS, emitters in solar cells, drift regions in power devices (low for high breakdown), and the resistivity of substrates ().

Pitfall

Compensation: adding acceptors to an n-type sample reduces but increases impurity scattering, so mobility drops — net doping and total impurities are not the same.
