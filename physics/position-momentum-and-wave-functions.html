Engineering applications

Fourier duality between position and momentum is the same mathematics as time–frequency in signals (Fourier transform) and as near-field/far-field in optics: a narrow slit (or a short pulse) means a broad angular (or spectral) spread. Electron-beam lithography resolution and the diffraction limit both follow from it.
