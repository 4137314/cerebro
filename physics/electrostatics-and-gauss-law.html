Engineering applications

Capacitive touch screens and MEMS accelerometers, ESD and high-voltage insulation design (field crowding at sharp edges), electrostatic precipitators, depletion regions of pn junctions (Poisson's equation with dopant charge).

Pitfall

Gauss's law is always true but only useful with symmetry: for a finite line or a square plate the field is not constant on any simple surface.
