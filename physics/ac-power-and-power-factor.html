Worked example

Motor 10 kW at PF 0.7 lagging on 230 V, 50 Hz: A, kvar. Correct to 0.95: kvar, µF; new current 45.8 A — line losses drop by 45%.

Engineering applications

Industrial PF correction (utilities charge for reactive energy), active PFC in switch-mode supplies (EU harmonic limits), inverter sizing in VA, reactive-power control for grid voltage support.

Pitfall

PF also includes harmonic distortion: a rectifier load with in-phase but distorted current has PF < 1 even with (distortion factor).
