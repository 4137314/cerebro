Engineering applications

Every field solver (FEM, FDTD) discretises these operators; Stokes underlies Ampère-loop current probes (Rogowski coils) and Faraday induction; divergence underlies charge conservation in circuit simulators (KCL is integrated).

Pitfall

In curvilinear coordinates the unit vectors depend on position: you cannot differentiate components as if Cartesian.
