Engineering applications

Voltage conversion and isolation in power grids and supplies (distribution), flyback/forward converters (switching converters), impedance matching (baluns, RF transformers), current transformers for metering, galvanic isolation in gate drivers.

Pitfall

Transformers do not work at DC: a DC component saturates the core ( collapses). And the ideal current relation fails at light load, where the magnetising current dominates.
