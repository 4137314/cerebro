#+TITLE: cerebro issues
#+STARTUP: overview

Personal, non-proprietary issue tracker: plain Org headings, no
external service, no database. Picked up by org-agenda-files (see
.emacs.d/lisp/cerebro-org.el). Create new entries with
=bin/cerebro-issue "title" [tag1 tag2 ...]= or =M-x org-capture=;
browse with =C-c i= (see .emacs.d/lisp/cerebro-issues.el).
