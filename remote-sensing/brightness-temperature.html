Intuition

Brightness temperature is "how hot it looks"; kinetic temperature is "how hot it is". They coincide only for a perfect emitter.

Pitfall

Comparing thermal images of surfaces with different emissivity (water, rock, metal roofs) as if they were temperatures mislabels cool materials as hot and vice versa.
