Digital images

A digital image is an matrix of numbers (pixels); in remote sensing each value is the digital number of a ground cell in one band, and a multiband image is a stack of such matrices.

Intuition

For the computer an image is just a table of measurements; "seeing" it requires mapping those numbers to screen brightness.

Pitfall

Integer arithmetic on DNs overflows or truncates ( differences, ratios): convert to floating point before computing indices or statistics.
