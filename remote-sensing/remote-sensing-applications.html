Applications of remote sensing

Optical and radar remote sensing with automatic image analysis serve environmental monitoring, risk analysis and civil protection, climate change, natural resources, agriculture and food security, forestry, urban areas, the cryosphere, meteorology and planetary exploration; each pairs specific sensors with specific processing.

Intuition

The same chain — acquisition, processing, recognition — answers very different questions; what changes is the choice of wavelength, revisit, resolution and algorithm for the phenomenon at hand.

Pitfall

A product is only useful if validated against ground truth in the area and season where it is used: maps transferred to new regions without validation can be confidently wrong.
