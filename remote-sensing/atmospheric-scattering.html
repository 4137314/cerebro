Definition

  • Rayleigh: scatterers (molecules) much smaller than ; intensity . Blue (0.45 µm) is scattered about times more than red (0.7 µm): blue sky, bluish haze in images.
  • Mie: scatterers of size comparable to (aerosol, smoke, dust); dependence on weaker, forward scattering.
  • Non-selective: scatterers much larger than (cloud droplets): all visible wavelengths equally, so clouds look white.

Effect on the measurement

The radiance at the sensor is

where is path radiance: light scattered into the line of sight without touching the target. It adds an offset, larger in the blue, that reduces contrast; scattered skylight also illuminates shadows.

Intuition

Scattering is fog in the image: it adds light that carries no information about the ground, mostly at short wavelengths.

Pitfall

Because scattering is strongest in the blue, the blue band of a multispectral image has the largest offset and the lowest contrast; dark-object subtraction exploits exactly this (see corrections).
