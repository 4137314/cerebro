SAR polarimetry

A fully polarimetric radar measures the complex scattering matrix of each cell; decompositions such as Pauli separate surface (single-bounce), double-bounce and volume scattering, which map naturally onto bare soil and water, buildings and tree trunks, and vegetation canopies.

Definition

The scattering matrix relates transmitted and received fields in the H/V basis; in the monostatic case . Acquisition modes: single-pol (one channel), dual-pol (e.g. VV+VH, Sentinel-1), quad-pol (all four, RADARSAT-2, Biomass).

Pauli decomposition:

  • : odd-bounce (surface) scattering — blue;
  • : even-bounce (dihedral) scattering — red;
  • : volume scattering (random orientations) — green.

The RGB composite reads directly as soil/water, buildings, vegetation.

Intuition

A wave hitting a flat surface keeps its polarization; bouncing twice in a corner flips the sign between H and V; a tangle of branches scrambles it. Measuring all combinations tells the mechanisms apart.

Pitfall

A building rotated with respect to the radar's look direction no longer acts as a clean dihedral and shows up as "volume" scattering; orientation-angle compensation is needed before interpreting decompositions.
