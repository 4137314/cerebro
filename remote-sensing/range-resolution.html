Consequences

  • resolution does not degrade with altitude: it depends only on the pulse;
  • it degrades towards near range (small ) and is undefined at nadir (), another reason to look sideways;
  • a finer resolution needs a shorter pulse, hence higher peak power for the same energy — solved by chirp pulses.

Intuition

Range resolution is half the pulse length in metres: echoes from points closer than that blur into one.

Pitfall

Ground-range resolution varies across the swath: the pixel spacing of a ground-range product is constant, its true resolution is not.
