Statement

Derivation

The beam illuminates along-track a strip of width . Two targets within that strip return echoes during the same pulses and cannot be distinguished by timing. With diffraction-limited beamwidth the result follows.

Example

C band, cm, km, m: km. To get 5 m one would need km. Airborne at km with m: about 190 m.

Intuition

A real antenna is a torch: the farther the wall, the wider the spot, and everything inside the spot looks like one point.

Pitfall

Increasing improves the resolution but narrows the beam and the swath; the way out is not a bigger antenna but a synthetic one.
