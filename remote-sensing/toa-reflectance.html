From DN to top-of-atmosphere and surface reflectance

Calibration turns DN into at-sensor radiance ; dividing by the solar irradiance on a horizontal surface gives the top-of-atmosphere reflectance ; removing path radiance and transmittance with an atmospheric model gives surface reflectance, the quantity comparable across dates and sensors.

Method

  1. Radiance: (W m sr µm), gain and offset from the metadata.
  2. TOA reflectance:

with the mean exo-atmospheric solar irradiance of the band, the Earth–Sun distance in astronomical units, the solar zenith angle.

  1. Surface reflectance: inverting the at-sensor model of a Lambertian surface,

with the transmittances, diffuse sky irradiance, path radiance, estimated by radiative transfer codes or by dark-object subtraction.

Example

Red band, W m sr µm, , , : . Sentinel-2 Level-1C products are TOA reflectance (scaled by 10 000), Level-2A surface reflectance.

Intuition

Reflectance answers "what fraction of the light that arrived came back?", which removes the Sun's strength, angle and distance from the measurement.

Pitfall

TOA reflectance still contains the atmosphere: indices and thresholds tuned on surface reflectance shift when applied to TOA data, most in the blue.
