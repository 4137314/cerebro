Speckle reduction

Speckle is reduced by multilooking (averaging independent looks, at the cost of resolution) or by adaptive filters such as Lee, Kuan and Frost, which in homogeneous areas average strongly and near edges and point targets keep the original value, by comparing the local coefficient of variation with the speckle's.

Example

Intuition

Smooth only where the variation can be explained by speckle alone; where there is more variation than speckle could produce, there must be structure, so leave it.

Pitfall

Every speckle filter trades noise against resolution and radiometric fidelity: filtered intensities can be biased, so estimate on homogeneous areas of unfiltered (or carefully multilooked) data.
