Statistical (rank) filters

Nonlinear filters replace each pixel with a statistic of its window — median, mode, minimum, maximum, variance; the median removes impulse noise while preserving edges, the mode cleans classification maps, variance and range measure local texture.

Method

For each pixel, sort the values of the window and output

  • median: middle value; an isolated outlier never wins, and a straight edge keeps its position;
  • mode: most frequent value; used on label maps (majority filter) to remove isolated misclassified pixels;
  • min / max: erosion / dilation in grey-level morphology;
  • variance, range, entropy: local texture measures (see texture).

Cost: per pixel by sorting, with histogram updates.

Example

A window with values : mean 37 (outlier spread around), median 11 (outlier removed).

Intuition

Ranking makes a filter robust: a few crazy values cannot move the middle of a sorted list.

Pitfall

The median erodes thin lines and sharp corners narrower than half the window: roads one pixel wide disappear with a median.
