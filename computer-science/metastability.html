Metastability and synchronizers

When a flip-flop samples an input that changes inside its setup–hold window it may hang between 0 and 1 for an unbounded time, resolving with probability that decays exponentially, ; a chain of two flip-flops gives the first one a whole clock period to resolve, raising the mean time between failures to years.

Construction

Multi-bit values cannot be synchronised bit by bit (bits may resolve in different cycles): use a handshake, Gray-coded counters or an asynchronous FIFO.

Intuition

A ball balanced on a hill eventually rolls to one side; the longer you wait before looking, the less likely it is still on top.

Pitfall

Metastability cannot be eliminated, only made improbable; and the synchronised signal is valid one or two cycles late, which protocols must accept.
