Recurring state-machine patterns

A few fragments recur in every controller: busy-wait (a self-loop until a signal arrives), choice (branch on a signal), fire-and-check (start a counter or slow unit, then wait for its terminal count), edge detection (wait for 1, then for 0) and the one-cycle pulse; Moore versions react one cycle late and can miss short pulses, conditional (Mealy) outputs react at once; the simplest any-edge detector is one flip-flop and an XOR.

Intuition

Let the datapath count and wait; let the state machine only decide when to start, stop and look.

Pitfall

A Moore machine samples its inputs once per cycle: a signal must last longer than a clock period to be seen with certainty, and the reaction comes one cycle after it. Inputs not synchronous with the clock must be synchronized before any of these patterns can rely on them.
