Voltage scaling, DVFS and parallel datapaths

Lowering cuts energy quadratically but slows the gates; parallel or pipelined datapaths recover the throughput at the lower voltage, so a duplicated unit at half frequency and reduced voltage can do the same work for roughly 40% of the power; DVFS adapts voltage and frequency to the current load.

Intuition

Two slow, relaxed workers use less energy than one fast, stressed worker, because speed costs voltage and voltage costs its square.

Pitfall

Below a certain voltage leakage dominates and the slower circuit leaks for longer: energy per operation has a minimum, and scaling further makes things worse.
