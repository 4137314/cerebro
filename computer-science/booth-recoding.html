Intuition

Long runs of ones become "subtract at the start, add at the end", and looking at bits two at a time halves the rows to add.

Pitfall

Partial products are signed: each row must be sign-extended to the full width (or use the sign-extension-prevention trick with constant ones), otherwise the high half of the product is wrong.
