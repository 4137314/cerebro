Intuition

A NAND is an AND followed by an inverter, and also an OR with inverted inputs; between two levels the two inversions annihilate.

Pitfall

Forgetting to invert the single literals feeding the output NAND gives where was meant. In CMOS, inverting gates are cheaper (4 transistors for a 2-input NAND, 6 for an AND), so NAND–NAND is usually smaller and faster than AND–OR, not just "more uniform".
