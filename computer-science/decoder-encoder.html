Intuition

Decoding spreads a dense code over many wires (one wire per value); encoding packs the wires back into a few bits — and the priority version decides what to do when more than one wire claims the bus, as interrupt controllers do.

Pitfall

A plain encoder gives nonsense when two inputs are active ( and together give , "3"); that is why the priority version exists, and why the plain one is designed treating multi-hot inputs as don't-cares.
